# Manuscript review gaps — 20 October 2026

This is a research decision record for [`manuscript.md`](manuscript.md), not a claim of acceptance or a replacement for human peer review. The primary VitaminC result is complete under its local freeze. The sparse edit-text and released ALBERT checkpoint comparisons, along with a 24-error inspection, were completed afterward and are transparently supplementary.

## Current scientific claim

The frozen, paired comparison supports a +0.0891 AUROC gain for the **combined** deterministic GMEE text-change descriptors added to an eight-feature lexical logistic classifier on the released, model-preselected VitaminC factual-revision candidate pool. Separately frozen post-primary same-row comparisons scored 0.8205 AUROC for sparse edit TF-IDF and 0.9189 for the authors' released ALBERT checkpoint, both above the 0.8116 augmented model. Thus the contribution cannot be framed as a new best factual-revision detector or as proof that the typed rules beat learned text features. The checkpoint was evaluated as released, not retrained; its comparison is descriptive because it was planned after the original result.

The paper could contribute a careful **component and reproducibility study**: a low-dimensional deterministic representation, honest matched contrasts, explicit cross-split overlap quarantine, byte-pinned local analysis, grouped uncertainty, and transparent null/negative comparisons to richer baselines. Whether that is enough for a particular venue remains unknown. The paper does not establish novelty for claim evolution or full GMEE, and it has no new human annotation dataset.

## Viable directions

1. **Narrow component study.** Report the lexical gain, stronger sparse baseline, and much stronger same-row released checkpoint with the integrity audit. A short empirical/replication venue or workshop may be a better fit than a broad method claim. Acceptance and novelty remain unknown until a venue and external review are chosen.
2. **Stronger explanatory component study.** Confirm the observed complementary descriptor value beyond sparse text on a prospectively planned untouched evaluation source, and investigate individual feature families with appropriate evidence. If no incremental value remains, report that negative finding rather than optimizing against the exposed VitaminC test.
3. **Full natural-news study.** Build independent human gold and event-disjoint splits for the actual GMEE relatedness/type/span/context targets. This is the path for the originally envisioned system paper, but the necessary data collection and rights work are open.
